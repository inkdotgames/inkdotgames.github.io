Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

The short version: Loose Ends does not collect, store or share any personal information. Your game progress and settings are saved only on your own device.

Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Loose Ends: Arrow Puzzle (the "Game"), published by Inkdot Games ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at yanghd.game.dev@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not send any data to our servers or to third parties. The Game does not even request permission to access the internet.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the Game saves a small amount of data locally on your device:

DataPurpose
Current level and highest unlocked levelResume your progress
Number of hints availableGameplay
Sound and vibration settingsRemember your preferences
Whether the tutorial has been shownAvoid repeating it

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its app data.

Device permissions

On Android, the Game may request the Vibrate permission to provide haptic feedback. You can turn vibration off at any time in the Game's settings. The Game does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

App stores and platform providers

The Game is distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store. These platforms may collect information (for example, download statistics or crash reports, depending on your device settings) under their own privacy policies, which we do not control:

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy

Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for a general audience. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Your rights

Since we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove all locally stored game data by uninstalling the Game. If you have questions about your privacy rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game adds features that collect data (for example advertising or purchases), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
